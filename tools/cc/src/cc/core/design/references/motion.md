# Motion and helpful personality

Use motion to reveal state, causality, spatial continuity or progress. Choose a
small set of timings and easing from the product's existing motion system. Measure
actual responsiveness; do not delay a frequent operation for a decorative entrance.
Avoid adding motion to every section simply to make the page feel finished.

Implement and inspect prefers-reduced-motion and platform accessibility settings.
Content and controls must remain visible and usable when animation is disabled,
interrupted or fails. Preserve focus, reading order and input during transitions;
test repeated clicks, rapid navigation and a slow device. Account for motion
sensitivity and flashing content according to the applicable requirements.

Delight should help the user: clear confirmation, recovery that preserves work,
useful shortcuts, or language and imagery grounded in the product. A surprise
effect must not conceal state or make an ordinary control behave unpredictably.
Do not require a reward animation or whimsical voice for a serious workflow.

Inspect the actual animation in the browser or native runtime. Still screenshots
cannot establish motion quality, keyboard continuity or frame performance. Record
the tested environment, observed behavior and reduced-motion result for QA; use
the smallest relevant artifact and preserve explicit gaps.
